  • Reading and searching files stored in Google Drive

Google user data is accessed only as necessary to perform tasks requested or configured by the owner of Zorya-Hermes.

Information accessed by Zorya-Hermes may be processed by software and service providers required to operate the AI agent, including AI model services, solely for the purpose of carrying out owner-requested tasks.

Data Sharing

Zorya-Hermes does not sell Google user data or share it for advertising, marketing, or unrelated purposes.

Data may only be transmitted to service providers that are necessary to perform the automation or AI-processing tasks requested by the owner.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data created in Google Drive, Google Sheets, or Google Docs remains under the control of the Google account owner and may be deleted by the owner at any time.

Authorization granted to Zorya-Hermes may also be revoked by the owner through the owner's Google Account security settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy
